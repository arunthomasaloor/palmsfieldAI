The four we get asked for most

What we actually build.

Every business is a little different, but the same four keep coming up. Each one has a number attached to it before we start, so you can tell whether it worked.

AI phone answering and missed call recovery

Picks up 24/7 · books into your calendar

A call that rings out is usually a job somebody else got. A voice agent answers when your team cannot, handles the questions you get asked fifty times a week, and puts the appointment straight into your scheduler.

  • Covers after hours, weekends, and the times every line is busy
  • Texts back within seconds on anything that still slips through
  • Writes up what was said and files it where you keep your leads
The one with the clearest before and after.

A front office that works in English and Spanish

Both languages · calls, texts, and paperwork

Plenty of your customers would rather do business in Spanish, and plenty of crews would rather read the work order in Spanish too. Everything we build can run in both without you hiring for it.

  • Calls and chats answered in whichever language the customer opens with
  • Work orders, safety notes, and procedures translated for the crew
  • Quotes and follow-ups written in the language the customer prefers
Hard to find locally. Easy for us to include.

An assistant that answers from your own documents

Your manuals, contracts, and price book

Every office has one person who knows where everything is, and everyone else interrupts them. This gets the rest of the team the same answer without walking over to that desk.

  • Equipment manuals, warranty terms, and whatever the price is this month
  • Leases, vendor agreements, and owner contracts
  • Every answer points back at the page it came from, so it can be checked
Stops your best person being a search engine.

Paperwork into your software without the retyping

Invoices, work orders, intake forms

The job nobody wants: reading a document and typing what it says into a different screen. A photo or a PDF goes in and clean fields come out the other side.

  • Invoices, bills of lading, purchase orders, and permits keyed automatically
  • A photo of an equipment nameplate pulls the model, serial, and warranty status
  • A voice memo from the truck comes back as a written estimate
Usually the easiest one to prove in a week.

Common questions

What owners ask on the first call.

Do we need someone technical on staff?

No. Most of the businesses we work with don't have an IT person at all. We build around the tools you already use and train whoever runs the office day to day.

Which software do you connect to?

Whatever you're running: your software, scheduling system, spreadsheets, email or accounting package. We'd rather fit your setup than ask you to replace it.

What does it cost?

The workflow review is a flat rate. Builds are quoted after we've seen the work, since scope varies a lot between businesses. We'll talk real numbers on the first call.

Will this put someone out of a job?

That's not what we're for, and we'll say so on the call. Every business we've looked at has more work than hours. Automating the retyping means the same people spend their time on customers instead.

What if it breaks after you leave?

Everything we build comes with 60 days of support, and the handover includes documentation plus a trained owner on your staff. After that, monthly support is optional.

How fast do we see something working?

The review gives you a written plan within a day. A first automation is usually running within a few weeks, depending on how your systems are set up.

Do you work outside San Antonio?

Bexar County and the surrounding towns are covered with no travel charge. Farther out we take on selectively, usually structured as one on-site day plus remote follow-up.
